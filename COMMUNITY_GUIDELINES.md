# Community Guidelines – QuizPoll Community

**Mission:** Foster a welcoming, inspiring, and high-quality educational environment where learners, students, and educators share knowledge through interactive quizzes and polls.

---

## 1. Respect & Civil Discussion
* **Be Kind and Courteous:** Disagreements regarding quiz answers or poll outcomes should be constructive and respectful.
* **No Harassment or Bullying:** We do not tolerate name-calling, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, or personal attacks on any community member.
* **Zero Tolerance for Hate Speech:** Content that attacks, degrades, or dehumanizes individuals based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, age, or sexual orientation will result in an immediate permanent ban.

---

## 2. Quiz & Poll Quality Standards
* **Accurate & Educational:** Strive for factual accuracy in questions, answers, and explanations. 
* **Clear Explanations:** When creating quizzes, include an explanation for why the correct option is right. This enriches the learning experience for all members.
* **Balanced Poll Options:** Provide neutral, well-defined poll choices. Avoid biased or intentionally misleading polls.

---

## 3. Prohibited Content
* **Spam & Self-Promotion:** Do not post duplicate quizzes, spam comments, affiliate links, or promotional URLs in discussion feeds.
* **Cheating & Exploitation:** Do not share quiz question answer keys in comment sections to undermine leaderboard integrity.
* **Sexual Content & Violence:** Any sexually suggestive, graphic, or violent textual content will be removed immediately.
* **Impersonation:** Do not pose as other community members, moderators, or staff.

---

## 4. Reporting & Moderation
* **Flagging Inappropriate Content:** Every quiz, poll, and comment features a "Report" action in the overflow menu. When content is reported:
  - It is flagged for automated and human moderator review.
  - Accounts with multiple verified violations face progressive discipline: warning, temporary suspension, or permanent account ban.
* **User Blocking:** You can block any member from their profile or via comment actions. Blocked users will not be able to interact with your quizzes or view your discussion feed posts.
