QuizPoll Community Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · App ID: com.aistudio.quizpoll.qpxmrk

QuizPoll Community processes account identifiers, profile details, quizzes, polls, posts, comments, votes, quiz attempts, bookmarks and notification metadata to provide its community features.

Storage

The app does not provide user photo, video, audio or document uploads to cloud media storage. Community content is structured text and metadata stored in Cloud Firestore. Local Room and DataStore are used for device-side cache and settings.

Advertising

The selected ad partners are Google AdMob and InMobi through AdMob Mediation. Google Mobile Ads SDK, InMobi mediation adapter and Google UMP may process advertising identifiers, device/app information, network information, ad requests and consent signals for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Ad delivery depends on account approval and mediation configuration.

Consent

UMP consent information is refreshed when the app launches. Ads are requested only when UMP reports that requests are allowed. Publisher messages and mediation partners must be configured in AdMob before production.

Account deletion

Users can delete their account in app settings. Users who cannot access the app can email rohitpandit55970@gmail.com from their registered email address. Requests are identity-verified before processing.
